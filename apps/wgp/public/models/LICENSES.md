| File | What |
|---|---|
| `hero.glb` | The Node, the in-game hero: 16,000 triangles, a 53-bone skeleton, no textures. Medium and high tiers. |
| `hero-lo.glb` | The same body at 5,000 triangles, for the low tier. |
| `hero-anims.glb` | The hero's motion: 13 motion-capture clips (idle, walk, jog, sprint, jumps, landing, punches, hits, a fall) on the same skeleton, no mesh. |
| `cast-<id>.glb` | The rest of the cast (Aunt Mainnet, Uncle Gen, Mary JPEG, J. Jonah Bearson, Gwei, Gas Goblin, Sandwich Man, Doc Octochain, The Sybil), each a MakeHuman body on the same skeleton: about 7–8k triangles ("Body") plus a 2.6–3.4k one ("BodyLo"), no textures. Medium and high tiers. |
| `cast-<id>-lo.glb` | "BodyLo" alone, for the low tier. |
| `cast-anims.glb` | The cast's extra motion: 6 clips (interact, crouch idle, spell idle and cast, sword stance, treading water) on the same skeleton, no mesh. |
| `civ-<id>.glb` | The city's people (office worker, jogger, retiree, teenager, shopper, builder, student, child): eight MakeHuman bodies on the same skeleton, aged about 9 to 75, slim to heavy, each a full body ("Body", 2,400–3,000 triangles) and a light one ("BodyLo", 900–1,350), fingers folded into the hands, no textures. The high tier. Each person's colours (skin, hair, clothes) are picked in the game from lists in the file. |
| `civ-<id>-lo.glb` | "BodyLo" alone, for the low and medium tiers. |
| `crowd-anims.glb` | The crowd's motion: 13 clips on the same skeleton, no mesh (standing, talking, a formal walk, a dance, and moves made on the idle and the walk: wave, cheer, fist pump, pointing up, filming on a phone, looking at a phone standing and walking, clapping, looking up). |

The two hero bodies are generated by `launch/brand/hero/export.py --game` from `launch/brand/hero/build.py`'s body; the cast's by `launch/brand/hero/build_cast.py` from the table in `launch/brand/hero/cast.py`; the civilians' by the same script from `launch/brand/hero/civilians.py`, and their clips by `launch/brand/hero/crowd_anims.py`. The clips are retargeted onto that skeleton by `launch/brand/hero/retarget.py` (`--cast` for the cast's). Every file is then packed for the wire by `apps/wgp/scripts/models/compress.sh` (EXT_meshopt_compression, float data rounded to 16 significant bits; `apps/wgp/scripts/models/verify.mjs` checks a packed set against the originals). Nothing here is hand-edited.

## The human body, skeleton and skin weights: CC0

The body is made with MPFB 2.0 (MakeHuman Plugin For Blender) from MakeHuman's system assets, which ship inside the extension: the base mesh, the macro and detail targets, and the `game_engine` rig with its weights.

- `3dobjs/base.obj`: "This asset was explicitly released as CC0 in September 2020." The copyright holders at release were Data Collection AB, Joel Palmius and Jonas Hauquier.
- `rigs/standard/weights.game_engine.json`: `"license": "CC0"`.
- The targets and rig definitions are part of the same CC0 MakeHuman release.

CC0 1.0: https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/. Characters made from these assets are free to use, modify and ship, commercially or not, with no attribution required. We credit MakeHuman anyway.

## The motion: Quaternius' Universal Animation Library, CC0

`hero-anims.glb`, `cast-anims.glb` and `crowd-anims.glb` are retargeted from Quaternius' *Universal Animation Library (Standard)*, released under CC0 1.0 (`License.txt` in the pack). The crowd's wave, cheer, fist pump, pointing, phone and clapping moves are the UAL idle and walk with the arms and head re-posed by `crowd_anims.py`: derived from CC0 clips, and our own work on top.
- Source: https://quaternius.com/packs/universalanimationlibrary.html (mirrored at https://opengameart.org/content/universal-animation-library).
- Archive SHA-256: `18ff1a7215f4852b320203e8aaf02a1578b5c8eef9027fbaedfcedc7b85a3ac2`. The owner approved fetching it on 2026-11-02; see `launch/brand/hero/mocap/SOURCE.md`.

No attribution is required. We credit Quaternius anyway.

## The tool: MPFB, GPL-3.0-or-later (not shipped)

MPFB's Python code only runs inside Blender to generate the model. None of it is copied into, linked from or shipped with the game, and output made with a GPL tool isn't covered by the tool's licence.

## Our work

The suit (toe boxes, smoothing, the web pattern's coordinates, the baked shading data), the mask lenses traced from our own sticker art, and the export are original work for this project, under the project's own terms. The hero is a parody of our own: no Marvel suit details and no spider emblem.

The cast's clothes, hair, props and head pieces (the bear, the burger, the goblin, the hoods, the glider, Doc's harness) are generated from primitives and metaballs by `build_cast.py`, and their painted faces are decals in the game's shader laid out from our own sticker art. No clothing, hair or prop asset packs are used. All of it is original work for this project, and the characters are our own parodies.

The civilians are made the same way, released by us under CC0 1.0 like the MakeHuman bodies under them: their clothes, hair, hats, glasses, bags, backpacks and phones are generated by `build_cast.py` from `civilians.py`, and they share one painted face. They are generic passers-by, not likenesses of anyone.
