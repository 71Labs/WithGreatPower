# Licences for public/models

| File | What |
|---|---|
| `hero.glb` | The Node, the in-game hero: 16,000 triangles, a 53-bone skeleton, no textures. Medium and high tiers. |
| `hero-lo.glb` | The same body at 5,000 triangles, for the low tier. |
| `hero-anims.glb` | The hero's motion: 13 motion-capture clips (idle, walk, jog, sprint, jumps, landing, punches, hits, a fall) on the same skeleton, no mesh. |

The two bodies are generated by `launch/brand/hero/export.py --game` from `launch/brand/hero/build.py`'s body. The clips are retargeted onto that skeleton by `launch/brand/hero/retarget.py`. Nothing here is hand-edited.

## The human body, skeleton and skin weights: CC0

The body is made with MPFB 2.0 (MakeHuman Plugin For Blender) from MakeHuman's system assets, which ship inside the extension: the base mesh, the macro and detail targets, and the `game_engine` rig with its weights.

- `3dobjs/base.obj`: "This asset was explicitly released as CC0 in September 2020." The copyright holders at release were Data Collection AB, Joel Palmius and Jonas Hauquier.
- `rigs/standard/weights.game_engine.json`: `"license": "CC0"`.
- The targets and rig definitions are part of the same CC0 MakeHuman release.

CC0 1.0: https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/. Characters made from these assets are free to use, modify and ship, commercially or not, with no attribution required. We credit MakeHuman anyway.

## The motion: Quaternius' Universal Animation Library, CC0

`hero-anims.glb` is retargeted from Quaternius' *Universal Animation Library (Standard)*, released under CC0 1.0 (`License.txt` in the pack).
- Source: https://quaternius.com/packs/universalanimationlibrary.html (mirrored at https://opengameart.org/content/universal-animation-library).
- Archive SHA-256: `18ff1a7215f4852b320203e8aaf02a1578b5c8eef9027fbaedfcedc7b85a3ac2`. The owner approved fetching it on 2026-11-02; see `launch/brand/hero/mocap/SOURCE.md`.

No attribution is required. We credit Quaternius anyway.

## The tool: MPFB, GPL-3.0-or-later (not shipped)

MPFB's Python code only runs inside Blender to generate the model. None of it is copied into, linked from or shipped with the game, and output made with a GPL tool isn't covered by the tool's licence.

## Our work

The suit (toe boxes, smoothing, the web pattern's coordinates, the baked shading data), the mask lenses traced from our own sticker art, and the export are original work for this project, under the project's own terms. The hero is a parody of our own: no Marvel suit details and no spider emblem.
