# Licences for launch/brand/hero

## The human body, skeleton and skin weights: CC0

The hero's body is generated by MPFB (MakeHuman Plugin For Blender) 2.0.18 from MakeHuman's
system assets, which ship inside the extension:
- the base mesh;
- the macro and detail targets;
- the `game_engine` rig and its weights.

The source files carry the release, as shipped in
`~/Library/Application Support/Blender/5.1/extensions/blender_org/mpfb/data/`:
- `3dobjs/base.obj`: "This asset was explicitly released as CC0 in September 2020." The copyright holders at release were Data Collection AB, Joel Palmius and Jonas Hauquier.
- `rigs/standard/weights.game_engine.json`: `"license": "CC0"`.
- The targets and rig definitions are part of the same CC0 MakeHuman asset release.

CC0 1.0 (public domain dedication): https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/.
Characters generated from these assets are free to use, modify and ship, commercially or not,
with no attribution required. We credit MakeHuman anyway.

## The tool: MPFB, GPL-3.0-or-later (not shipped)

MPFB's Python code is GPL-3.0-or-later. We only run it inside Blender to generate the model; none
of its code is copied into, linked from or shipped with the game or these assets. Output made
with a GPL tool isn't covered by the tool's licence.

## Our work: the project's own

Everything below is original work for this project and is under the project's own terms:
- the scripts in this folder;
- the suit modifications (the toe box, the smoothing, the web pattern);
- the mask lenses, taken from our own `../art/node.svg` sticker;
- the poses, renders, baked texture, `hero.glb` and previews.

The design is a parody hero of our own (no Marvel suit details, no spider emblem).

## The city backdrop: the project's own

`../art/city-backdrop.png` and `city-backdrop-alt.png` are frames of this game (WGP), rendered
by its own engine. They are captured headless with `../capture.mjs` and cropped.

## Not used

- No AI image generation.
- No downloaded models, textures or HDRIs.
- No assets with unclear licences.

The only network access is `preview.py` loading the header's Google Fonts (Bangers and Manrope,
SIL Open Font License), exactly as `../render.sh` does. Those fonts only appear in the preview
images.
